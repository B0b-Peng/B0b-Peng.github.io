How Story begins

My story of how I began on the road on physics

2024 Fall

I was admitted into Fudan Univ. through Gaokao, as the major of natural science. In the winter I selected chemistry as my official major, mostly because of my participation in the chemistry competition during high school, which allowed me to accumulate a lot of undergrad lower division level chemistry and helped me get good grades more easily in year-1 chemistry classes. And further I even scheduled some upper level division classes for the second semester (and the results turn out good at last then). I was attracted by physical chemistry and quantum chemistry so I learned and read some by myself. Now I know that at that time I was learning stat-mech and quantum mechanics actually.

2025 Spring

Out of interest I went to a professor developing quantum chemistry computation codes, specifically, on density functional theory. The professor told me his research involves much quantum mechanics and coding, which I studied for the remaining semester.

Later I was very shocked and amazed when learning about a real physics course like quantum mechanics. But I was also confused, also curious, about what the 'Hamiltonian' actually is. So I went to learn a bit analytic mechanics by video lectures. Furtherly I learned some electrodynamics too. Then I found myself absorbed in learning about physics and the mechanisms inside, instead of chemistry itself, by which I hoped to learn about it to study how reactions happen microscopically.

At this time, the thing that had most attracted me, was Einstein's theory of general relativity, which I first heard about through a scientific introductory video during a flight. Thus I used to set a goal for myself: if I remained a physics amateur, my physics road shall finally extend to GR. I was shockingly curious about how matters make spacetime curve! And I want to approach Einstein's great wisdom, by learning his great theory.

Finally on a May night I made up my mind to switch my major to physics. At this time I actually have little knowledge about what physics world looks like (not even now, but much better). I was only attracted, as if finding something that really belongs to me.

By some reason I chose an exchange program myself. I was not sure whether I can go due to visa issues, but I know it would be a great chance to enhance myself. I was accepted by Berkeley physics through BPIE program.

2025 Summer

I learned more carefully in electrodynamics with a textbook. I believed that with this, together with some poor QM, I can enter some group to start a research. I always thought that I want to be a theoretic man, not experimental, because I don't like experiments and prefer work like understanding concepts, computation, and framework building. I also read a little about GR through a very introductory book.

I didn't realize at all how faraway I was from theoretical physics frontiers! Yet I believed that in the future, I will enjoy being a designer, or an artist!

2025 Fall

After so many obstacles to come to Berkeley, I chose to take classes in analytic-mech, QM and stat-mech. Knowing the importance of communication, I talked to quite a few professors and one postdoc. Gradually, I got to know what theoretical physics is like (at least roughly).

For this whole semester I was busy learning GR as well as some other things, and later start on QFT. I learned a lot from all kinds of resources.

I believe it marks the real beginning of my physics learning, foreseeable a very long and tough road to go!

Two important events happen during the semester: Berkeley harvests 2025 Nobel Prize for Physics, and great Chinese physicist C.N. Yang passed away. It looks like there is little relation to me, but somehow I feel that it does have some relation to me. I feel inspired deeply as if something leads me, when I realized those two events when walking back home in the wind.

This is the beginning of my physics story.

There should be a luck or destiny that somehow leads me here, which encourages me a lot every time I recall this.

Written at the end of the influential year 2025, at Berkeley.
