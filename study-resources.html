Study Resources

I plan to put some of my organized study notes here and hope that they could help someone one day. They are mainly re-arranged course contents and I write them to enhance my understanding of specific topics that were too fragmentary at first learning.

Beware that some opinions and understanding might look naive from higher perspectives, but can work in the context temporarily.

  1. Supersymmetry study notes. Mainly from Prof. Fernando Quevedo's lectures, later I added other notes in. (finished but amending)
  2. Notes on the Standard Model. These include some aspects that are often not covered in a standard course of QFT or gauge theory. Including S-matrix, aspects of states, Yang-Mills and QCD, electroweak and Higgs, anomalies. (working)
  3. String theory, Polchinski volume 1, reading notes. Following the book, doing the exercises and adding supplementary explanations. (working)
  4. (plan) The way along Holography. Maybe this will start in Sept. and I plan to record the topics along my study on holography.
